Epistemic Provenance & Spatial Limitations

1. Regional Continental Prior Extrapolation

The open wet-chemistry training samples (AfSIS Phase I) are centered in West Africa. The median distance to our Mukono, Uganda site (0.3547° N, 32.7475° E) is approximately 2,533 km. Consequently, the model strictly functions as a regional continental prior extrapolation and must not be misrepresented as a local field calibration.

2. Proxy Telemetry Physics (No Fake NPK)

The sensor hardware reads proximal physical proxies: capacitive moisture, thermistor temperature, potentiometric glass pH, and ionic electrical conductivity. We do not claim direct physical NPK sensor prongs, avoiding the widespread pseudoscience of uncalibrated multi-parameter prongs.
